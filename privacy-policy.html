1. Who we are

Brigent Distribution Sdn Bhd (Registration No. 201601021602 (1192541-U)) ("Brigent", "we", "us") is a business-to-business distributor of CCTV and extra low voltage (ELV) electronics in Malaysia. This policy explains how we handle your personal data under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010 of Malaysia (PDPA), including when you message our WhatsApp business number or contact us through this website.

2. What personal data we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and the name shown on your WhatsApp profile.
  • The messages you send us, and any photos, documents or details you choose to share, such as your name, company, site, project or product questions.
  • Our replies, quotations and proposals prepared for you, and any hand-over of your conversation to our staff.
  • If you are an existing customer: your company name, contact details and account type held in our customer records.
  • Basic technical information, such as your IP address, that our web server may record when you visit this website.

3. How we use your personal data

  • To answer your enquiries about products, stock, prices, quotations and technical support.
  • To recognise whether your number belongs to an existing dealer or customer account, so we give you the right price and pass you to the right sales person.
  • To prepare quotations and solution proposals, which our staff may review and approve.
  • To pass your conversation to a colleague when you ask for a person or when our assistant cannot answer.
  • To follow up on enquiries and quotations you asked us about, and to handle warranty and support.
  • To keep business records and to meet legal requirements.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for unrelated purposes.

4. Automated assistant (AI)

The first replies on our WhatsApp business number are written by an automated assistant that uses artificial intelligence (AI) language models. It answers questions about our products, stock, prices, quotations and technical support. Our staff may read conversations to take over, to handle a hand-over, or to check quality. You can ask for a person at any time.

Please do not send sensitive information such as identity card numbers, bank or card details, or passwords.

5. Who we share your personal data with

  • Our own staff who need it to serve you.
  • Meta Platforms, Inc. (WhatsApp), which delivers the messages. WhatsApp handles messages under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Service providers that run our systems for us, such as cloud hosting and AI language-model providers that process message text to produce replies. We share only what is needed to provide the service.
  • Government or enforcement authorities, where the law requires it.

6. Processing outside Malaysia

Your data may be processed on servers outside Malaysia, for example by WhatsApp and by our AI service providers.

7. How long we keep your personal data

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above, for our business, warranty and accounting records, or as the law requires. After that we delete or anonymise it.

8. Security

We protect personal data by limiting which staff and systems can access it. No online service is completely secure, so please share only what is needed.

9. Your rights and choices

You may ask us to give you access to your personal data, to correct it, to stop sending you messages, or to withdraw your consent. To make a request, or to ask us to delete your data, contact us using the details in section 1 and tell us the phone number you used. We may need to confirm who you are first. We will reply within a reasonable time. We will delete or anonymise the data we are not required to keep.

10. Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed at anyone under 18.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page, with its effective date at the top.

12. Contact

For any question about this policy, contact Brigent Distribution Sdn Bhd, attention: Managing Director, at scott@brigent.com.my or +603-7866 0788.

2. Data peribadi yang kami kumpul

  • Nombor telefon WhatsApp anda dan nama yang dipaparkan pada profil WhatsApp anda.
  • Mesej yang anda hantar kepada kami, serta sebarang gambar, dokumen atau butiran yang anda pilih untuk kongsi, seperti nama, syarikat, tapak, projek atau soalan produk.

  • Jika anda pelanggan sedia ada: nama syarikat, butiran perhubungan dan jenis akaun anda dalam rekod pelanggan kami.
  • Maklumat teknikal asas, seperti alamat IP anda, yang mungkin direkodkan oleh pelayan web kami apabila anda melawat laman web ini.

3. Cara kami menggunakan data peribadi anda

  • Untuk menjawab pertanyaan anda tentang produk, stok, harga, sebut harga dan sokongan teknikal.
  • Untuk mengenali sama ada nombor anda milik akaun peniaga atau pelanggan sedia ada, supaya kami memberi harga yang betul dan menyerahkan anda kepada jurujual yang betul.
  • Untuk menyediakan sebut harga dan cadangan penyelesaian, yang mungkin disemak dan diluluskan oleh kakitangan kami.
  • Untuk menyerahkan perbualan anda kepada rakan sekerja apabila anda meminta seorang manusia atau apabila pembantu kami tidak dapat menjawab.
  • Untuk membuat susulan tentang pertanyaan dan sebut harga yang anda minta, serta mengendalikan waranti dan sokongan.
  • Untuk menyimpan rekod perniagaan dan memenuhi keperluan undang-undang.

Kami tidak menjual data peribadi anda, dan kami tidak menggunakannya untuk tujuan yang tidak berkaitan.

4. Pembantu automatik (AI)

Balasan pertama di nombor WhatsApp perniagaan kami ditulis oleh pembantu automatik yang menggunakan model bahasa kecerdasan buatan (AI). Ia menjawab soalan tentang produk, stok, harga, sebut harga dan sokongan teknikal kami. Kakitangan kami mungkin membaca perbualan untuk mengambil alih, mengendalikan penyerahan, atau menyemak kualiti. Anda boleh meminta seorang manusia pada bila-bila masa.

Sila jangan hantar maklumat sensitif seperti nombor kad pengenalan, butiran bank atau kad, atau kata laluan.

  • Kakitangan kami sendiri yang memerlukannya untuk melayani anda.

  • Pihak berkuasa kerajaan atau penguatkuasaan, apabila dikehendaki oleh undang-undang.

6. Pemprosesan di luar Malaysia

Data anda mungkin diproses pada pelayan di luar Malaysia, contohnya oleh WhatsApp dan pembekal perkhidmatan AI kami.

7. Tempoh kami menyimpan data peribadi anda

Kami menyimpan data peribadi hanya selama yang diperlukan untuk tujuan di atas, untuk rekod perniagaan, waranti dan perakaunan kami, atau seperti yang dikehendaki oleh undang-undang. Selepas itu kami memadam atau menganonimkannya.

Kami melindungi data peribadi dengan menghadkan kakitangan dan sistem yang boleh mengaksesnya. Tiada perkhidmatan dalam talian yang selamat sepenuhnya, jadi sila kongsi hanya apa yang diperlukan.
